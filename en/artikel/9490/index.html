From the curated archive of Disorder119. Two-piece lounge set by Jean Paul Gaultier from the Maille line, made in Italy. The ensemble consists of a short cut bodice with half-length sleeves and a softly falling pants with elastic waistband. The surface shows a finely structured, almost fried knit look with dense, compact feel. The clear black tone is subtly accentuated by contrasting, bright finishes on neckline, sleeves and hemline. The silhouette is reduced and close to the body without narrowing. The bodice ends slightly above the hip and creates a precise proportion to the loosely cut pants. A restrained, yet character-strong set with a calm presence and typical Gaultier design language.
