From the curated archive of Disorder119. A light, translucent top from the iconic Soleil line by Jean Paul Gaultier, which convinces with its characteristic materiality and subtle staging of transparency. The fine, flowing fabric lies softly over the silhouette and creates a reduced, almost floating effect. The design is accented by the striking lacing in the chest area with multi-coloured cords in warm tones, which give the piece an organic, almost artisanal touch. The loosely cut sleeves end in graphically elaborated details, reminiscent of abstracted sunbeams and take up the visual language of the Soleil collection. A piece that balances between lightness and expression and carries itself both as a layering element and as an independent statement.
