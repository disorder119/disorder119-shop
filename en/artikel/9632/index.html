From the curated archive of Disorder119. Clutch from the Maison Martin Margiela x H&M collaboration, which went on sale on 15 November 2012. The bag adopts one of the house's best-known motifs: it is shaped like a sweet wrapper – a flat, silver mirrored cushion with serrated, gathered ends. A weight marking '150 g e 5.29 OZ' and a barcode are embossed on the back, consistently continuing the packaging character. The opening is concealed in the upper seam and closes with a slim zipper; inside, the silver material continues, together with a white label bearing the row of numbers from 0 to 23 typical of Margiela. The material is thin and light, the surface mirrors and throws new reflections with every movement. The clutch is the H&M re-edition of the candy wrapper clutch Margiela had previously shown in its own collections. A playful, conceptual archive piece.
