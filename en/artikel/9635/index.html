Description

Long sleeve from the collaboration between Y-3 and the artist Chito, known for his airbrushed characters and motifs. According to the hangtag this is the model 'AOP GFX LS TEE' (article no. KQ8582, colour Black) from 2026. The black shirt is covered extensively with a white, softly blurred spray print: on the front, swirls, drips and a ring form a mask-like, face-like motif, on the back stands a large, roughly sprayed 'Y3', the sleeves carry flowing lines and splashes. The cut is oversized with deeply dropped shoulders, a wide body and long sleeves; the crew neck is ribbed. The Y-3 hangtags are still attached. An expressive archive piece that brings together street art and the reduced attitude of Yohji Yamamoto and adidas.
