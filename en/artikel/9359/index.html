From the curated archive of Disorder119. Asymmetrically cut skirt from the collaboration H&M x Maison Margiela in deep navy tone. The silhouette is deliberately unconventional, with flowing draping and set fabric guidance that gives the piece movement and a sculptural effect. The fabric falls soft and light, making the skirt work both in everyday life and in more conceptual stylings. Typical of Maison Margiela is the deconstructive approach to irregular lines and reduced, almost restrained coloration. A concise example of the translation of avant-garde design principles into a portable format.
