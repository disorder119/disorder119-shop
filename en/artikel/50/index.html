Description

Iconic mesh top by Jean Paul Gaultier with a complex all-over print that exemplifies the brand's visual language in the late 1990s and early 2000s. The finely punched mesh fabric shows a collage-like composition of floral motifs, graphic elements and figurative representations that link classical Gaultier themes such as art history, romance and body staging. The transparent materiality gives the top a light, close-to-body effect, while the sleeveless cut and the clear lines direct the focus completely on the print. Today, such multi-layered mesh tops are among the most sought-after archive pieces by Jean Paul Gaultier and have become significantly less frequent, especially in a well-preserved condition. A characteristic collector's item with high stylistic recognition.
