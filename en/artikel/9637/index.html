Description

These Y-3 ankle boots come from the early phase of the collaboration between Yohji Yamamoto and adidas; the inner label carries the production code 05/04 and the article number 551752. The upper combines smooth black leather on the forefoot with an elastic, neoprene-like shaft crossed by two white stripes on the side. A wide leather strap with a large metal buckle and Y-3 lettering wraps over the instep, and another Y-3 logo sits at the back of the shaft. The sole is unusual: a black rubber sole with white round studs and a ribbed profile under the forefoot, followed by a heel block with an embossed Y-3 monogram – a quotation of the football boot, translated into Yamamoto's reduced design language. An idiosyncratic archive piece from the first years of Y-3.
