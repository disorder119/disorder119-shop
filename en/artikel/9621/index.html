Description

Grass green knit vest by Issey Miyake, model Breeze Knit (article code IM58KE268 according to the label, identical to the code in the official Issey Miyake store). The chunky knit of 76% cotton and 24% polyester is firm and tactile, with strong ribbing at the V-neck, hem and pocket edge. The cut is deliberately oversized and long: the vest falls straight and boxy to below the hip, the armholes are cut very deep and wide – a design feature, not damage. On the front sits a single, very large, diagonally placed patch pocket with a rib trim that breaks the silhouette asymmetrically. No closure, to pull over. Made in Japan. A sculptural, colour-strong archive piece for layering over shirts, dresses or turtlenecks.
