Description

Thong sandals by Y-3, the line by Yohji Yamamoto and adidas. According to the box sticker this is the model 'Y-3 Boxing IV' (article no. 551733, colour code Black Y3/Star); the name refers to the Boxing line with which Y-3 once started. The sandals stand on a black, slightly raised rubber sole with orange tread panels embossed with the Y-3 monogram. The black footbed carries a Y-3 label in orange, the white leather-look strap is embossed with the lettering 'YOHJI YAMAMOTO' and runs into a black toe post with an orange embroidered Y-3 logo. The contrast of black, white and orange gives the simple sandal type a graphic, sporty touch. The sandals come with the original box and two Y-3 dust bags. An uncomplicated archive piece for summer.
