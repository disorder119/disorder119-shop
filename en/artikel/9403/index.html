From the curated archive of Disorder119. This iconic mesh blouse by Jean Paul Gaultier is an example of the house's experimental design language. The finely transparent mesh fabric forms the basis for a complex patchwork of graphic prints, point structures and organically-looking shapes in green, red, black and turquoise tones. The narrow, close-to-body silhouette is supported by the elastic material and ensures a precise, figure-emphasized fit. The contrasting sleeves with different patterns as well as the decorative bands in the front area give the piece a playful, yet avant-garde depth. A typical archive piece from the high phase of experimental JPG mesh aesthetics, versatile to wear as a statement piece or layering.
