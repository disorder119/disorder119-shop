Description

Fitted blazer from the Jean Paul Gaultier Femme line, made in Italy. On the outside the piece looks classic: black, lightly textured fabric with a fine crinkled character, notched lapels, three buttons, a welt chest pocket and a narrow waist with a slightly flared skirt. Small zippers at the side vents allow the hem to be opened. The inside is the real surprise: the entire lining is printed with white Chinese characters on a black ground, plus an inner pocket with zipper. A typical Gaultier play between strict tailoring and a hidden statement. A timeless archive piece that works as a classic blazer when closed and reveals its character when open.
