Description

Finely crafted leather blazer by Isaac Sellam in a cool grey-green with a matte, slightly washed surface. The shade moves between sage and stone, looks restrained and at the same time self-contained. The silhouette is waisted and clearly follows the body line. Precisely set seams structure the front and back and give the model an architectural calm. The lapel is classic, the front is closed by three buttons in warm brown tone. Patch pockets and long sleeves with distinctive button placket on the cuffs underline the craftsmanship. Back slits ensure freedom of movement and a balanced proportion. The leather shows a natural structure with subtle patina.
