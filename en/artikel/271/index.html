Description

Bag in the shape of a guitar case from the Maison Martin Margiela x H&M collaboration, which went on sale on 15 November 2012. The model takes up the guitar case bag Maison Martin Margiela had previously shown in one of its own collections and translates it into soft black leather – 'Leather' according to the hangtag. The silhouette follows the body and neck of a guitar, with stitched contours and a slightly padded feel. Silver-tone hardware defines the piece: D-rings, snap hooks and a detachable, length-adjustable webbing shoulder strap that carries the bag over the shoulder or across the body. Zippers with leather pulls open the body. The original hangtags are still attached: the white card with the row of numbers 0–23, 'Maison Martin Margiela Paris' and the H&M logo, plus a black leather tag and the multilingual material label. A conceptual, sculptural archive piece.
