From the curated archive of Disorder119. This dress by Walter Van Beirendonck shows the distinctive signature of the Belgian designer with a playful, yet conceptually clear approach. The long sleeves are made of different textures with floral motifs, dots and stripes, which gives the piece a collage-like effect.
