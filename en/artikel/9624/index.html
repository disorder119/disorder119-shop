Description

Black sweatshirt from the first phase of Y-3, the line by Yohji Yamamoto and adidas, with date code 12/03 on the care label. The sweater has a ribbed crew neck, raglan sleeves and wide rib cuffs at hem and sleeves that emphasise the slim cut. On the left sleeve sits a large tone-on-tone printed Y-3 logo that appears clearly or barely at all depending on the light. A small patch pocket with topstitching is worked in at the side. The inside is softly brushed, and the black label with orange Y-3 logo dates from the early years of the collaboration. An early, reduced archive piece from the founding year of the line.
