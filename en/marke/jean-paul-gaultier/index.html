Jean Paul Gaultier – Pre-Owned & Vintage

26 pieces available

Jean Paul Gaultier showed his first collection in Paris in 1976 and was soon known as the enfant terrible of French fashion. His signatures include the striped sailor top, the corset worn as outerwear and printed mesh tops with tattoo and op art motifs. Alongside the main line there were younger lines such as Junior Gaultier, Gaultier Jean’s and JPG by Gaultier. These lines from the 1980s and 1990s are especially sought after on the vintage market today.
