Maison Margiela – Pre-Owned & Vintage

The Belgian designer Martin Margiela founded his house in Paris in 1988. It is known for the white label with four stitches, the numbered lines from 0 to 23, the split-toe Tabi shoes and its work with reworked found garments. MM6 is the younger, more everyday line of the house. In 2012 a collection with H&M reissued earlier designs. MM6 and the H&M collection are listed under Maison Margiela in this shop.
