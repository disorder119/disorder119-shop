Y-3 – Pre-Owned & Vintage

28 pieces available

Y-3 is the joint line of Yohji Yamamoto and adidas, launched in 2002. The Y stands for Yamamoto, the 3 for the three adidas stripes. The collections combine sportswear with the Japanese designer’s wide, mostly black cuts. The line is best known for its sneakers, such as the Qasa, along with track jackets, trousers, bags and caps. Y-3 is regarded as one of the first lasting collaborations between a fashion designer and a sportswear company.
