Helmut Lang – Pre-Owned & Vintage

The Austrian designer Helmut Lang started out in Vienna and moved his label to New York in the late 1990s. His fashion shaped the minimalism of the 1990s: slim suits, denim, technical fabrics and details taken from military and workwear such as straps and harnesses. He left the company in 2005. Pieces from the years before are especially sought after by collectors.
