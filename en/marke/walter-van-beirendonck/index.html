Walter Van Beirendonck – Pre-Owned & Vintage

6 pieces available

Walter Van Beirendonck is one of the Antwerp Six, a group of graduates of the Royal Academy of Fine Arts in Antwerp who made Belgian fashion known internationally in the 1980s. His work is recognisable by strong colours, graphic motifs and humour. In the 1990s he designed the W.&L.T. line (Wild and Lethal Trash), whose pieces are collector’s items today. He headed the fashion department of the Antwerp academy for many years.
