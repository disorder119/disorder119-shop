Prada – Pre-Owned & Vintage

46 pieces available

Prada was founded in Milan in 1913 by Mario Prada as a leather goods shop. His granddaughter Miuccia Prada took over the house in the late 1970s and turned black nylon, until then a material for luggage and military equipment, into the signature of the brand. Prada Sport, later Linea Rossa, is recognisable by its red stripe and has combined technical fabrics with clean cuts since the late 1990s. Luna Rossa is the name of the Prada-backed sailing team in the America’s Cup; its clothing is listed under Prada in this shop as well.
