Balmain – Pre-Owned & Vintage

Pierre Balmain founded his house in Paris in 1945. In the years under Christophe Decarnin and then Olivier Rousteing, Balmain became known for strong shoulders, blazers with gold buttons, biker jeans and military-style jackets. Older pieces often carry the Pierre Balmain label, under which the house also ran ready-to-wear lines for a long time and which turns up frequently in vintage stock.
